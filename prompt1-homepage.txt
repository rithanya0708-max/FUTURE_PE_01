## prompt1:homepage

Create professional and attractive homepage content for Tasty Bites Restaurant in Coimbatore. 
The target audience includes students, families, and office employees.
The restaurant provides dine-in, takeaway, and home delivery services. 
Highlight the fresh and tasty food, affordable prices, fast and friendly service, convenient home delivery, comfortable dining venue, and family-friendly atmosphere. 
Create an attractive homepage with the restaurant name, an engaging headline, a short introduction, information about the services, the special features of the restaurant, and a strong call-to-action.
 Use a simple, friendly, professional, and attractive tone that encourages customers to visit Tasty Bites Restaurant.

Create an attractive homepage with:
- Restaurant name
- Engaging headline: "Coimbatore's Favorite Food Hub"
- Short introduction
- Attractive food/restaurant visuals
- Explore Our Menu button
- Book a Table button

The "Explore Our Menu" button should navigate to the Menu Highlights section/page.

The "Book a Table" button should navigate to the Booking/Contact section/page.

Use a simple, friendly, professional, and attractive tone that encourages customers to visit Tasty Bites Restaurant.

Make the homepage responsive and easy to navigate.
